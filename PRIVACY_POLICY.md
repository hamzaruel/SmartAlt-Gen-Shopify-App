# Privacy Policy — Alt Text Pro

Last Updated: June 2026

## 1. Introduction

Alt Text Pro (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides a Shopify app that helps merchants
generate and save clear, accessible alt text for the images in their store, for
better image SEO and accessibility. This Privacy Policy explains what
information we access, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## 2. Information We Access

To provide our service, the app may access:

- Store name and store URL
- Your product, collection, blog, and file images
- Basic product details used to describe images (such as title, type, and vendor)
- Permission to read those images and to save the alt text you approve back to
  your store

We only access the minimum information necessary for the app to function.

## 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Alt Text Pro:

- Does NOT collect customer personal data
- Does NOT access orders
- Does NOT access payment information
- Does NOT access customer names, emails, or addresses
- Does NOT track your store visitors

## 4. How We Use Information

We use store information only to:

- Scan your store to find which images need alt text
- Generate alt text from your product details or with AI
- Save the alt text you approve back to your store
- Provide support if you contact us

We do not sell, rent, or share merchant data with third parties.

## 5. Data Storage

We do not permanently store your images or any customer data. To keep the app
working while it is installed, we keep only a small amount of basic information,
such as a secure connection to your store and a simple count of how many AI
descriptions you have used. When the app is uninstalled, this information is
deleted and all access permissions are automatically revoked. The alt text
already saved to your store stays with you.

## 6. Third-Party Services

If you choose to generate alt text with AI, the image is sent to a trusted AI
provider (Google) to create a short description of what it shows. The image is
used only to generate that description and is not kept afterward. Their handling
of that request is subject to their own privacy policy.

## 7. Data Security

We follow reasonable security practices to protect store information and comply
with Shopify’s API requirements.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by
revising the “Last Updated” date above.
